# Activity inventory expansion audit — 20 August 2026

## Scope and decision rule

This audit expands RageRoom Directory beyond rage-room-only businesses without turning it into a general activities directory. A candidate was added only when a current official source identified the activity and a specific branch or a genuine mobile service. Venue-owned pages and their linked booking systems were preferred. Optional facts remain unknown where the source did not support them.

The 43-record starting inventory was deduplicated by name, address, postcode, domain, booking URL and phone before additions. Research produced 33 recorded candidate decisions: 25 new records, two enrichments to existing records, and six rejections. Multi-location operators were assessed branch by branch.

Twenty-five records were added, producing 68 total records. Twenty-two additions are fixed standalone/non-rage-room venues and three are mobile rage-room operators. Five new records carry multiple activity tags. Across the final inventory, 31 records carry more than one activity.

Fixed additions cover England (14), Scotland (2), Wales (3), and Northern Ireland (3), plus three UK-wide mobile services. The strongest remaining geographic gaps are current participatory car-smash evidence outside England; paint/splatter inventory in Wales and Northern Ireland; and standalone Tier 1 coverage in major markets such as Glasgow, Newcastle and Sheffield. These are research gaps, not an invitation to add weak evidence.
