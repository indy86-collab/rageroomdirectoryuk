# Rage Reset — sample-size guidance

Internal only. Do not hard-code into product behaviour.

| Signal | Guidance |
|---|---|
| First 25 sessions | Technical smoke only |
| 100 started sessions | Early gameplay signal |
| 250–500 started sessions | Reasonable initial product-direction signal |
| ≥ 7 days | Earliest return-rate review |
| 14 days | Preferred review period when traffic is low |

## Do not make large product changes on fewer than 100 starts unless

* Technical failure
* Severe stage drop-off
* Consistent direct user feedback
* Safety or privacy defect

See also decision gates in `launch-metrics.md` and conditional next actions in the Public Validation brief.
