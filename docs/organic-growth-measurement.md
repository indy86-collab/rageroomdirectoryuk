# Organic growth measurement baseline

Baseline recorded from Google Search Console on 4 August 2026 (latest available data at the time of the audit).

- Last 28 days: 6.33K clicks and 102K impressions.
- Generic/near-me query group: 1.19K clicks, up 321% versus the comparison period.
- Search audience: 97% UK, 100% non-branded in Search Console Insights.
- Image search: 5 clicks.
- Indexing report: 127 indexed and 67 not indexed; the latter included 33 crawled-currently-not-indexed, 21 canonical alternates, 10 noindex URLs, two duplicates without a selected canonical and one redirect.

## Checkpoints

At days 28, 56 and 90, record equivalent 28-day figures and compare page cohorts rather than only sitewide totals:

- `/near-me` and generic query cluster clicks, impressions, CTR and average position.
- Enriched listing pages versus listings not yet enriched.
- Priority city pages versus the remaining city cohort.
- Image-search clicks and image-sitemap discovery.
- Eligible indexed URLs, excluding deliberate noindex, redirects and canonical alternates.
- New relevant referring domains from verified venues and report coverage.

Targets for day 90: 20 enriched listings, 10 authorised multi-image galleries, eight relevant referring domains, at least 25 image clicks per 28 days, 25% growth in generic near-me clicks, and at least 85% indexation among eligible URLs.
