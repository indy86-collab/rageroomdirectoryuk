# Listing audit — 20 October 2026

Reviewed all 83 existing records and searched for missing rage-room, axe-throwing, paint-splatter and mobile experiences. Existing secondary categories (car smash, escape room, VR, archery and target activities) were checked against operator content where available. Added 44 venues, bringing the directory to 127; changed 20 existing records. No additional closure was sufficiently confirmed for deletion. The existing 26 blacklist entries remain excluded.

## Method and limits

Official operator pages and their booking links were preferred. HTTP reachability alone was not treated as proof of trading or closure. Existing verification dates were preserved unless a record was substantively rechecked; this pass does not certify every historical price, age restriction or opening hour. Unknown new fields remain null. No checkout was completed and no operator was contacted.

The machine-readable [audit](../data/listing-audit-2026-10-20.json) records every existing listing, outcome, source, and every new hero image’s source asset. Nine existing records have been set unverified where current operator evidence could not be established. Client-rendered booking shells and the third-party-only Leominster record require follow-up; historical details remain visible.

## Images

All 44 new listings have a locally stored cover from their official operator website. Covers were decoded, resized to at most 1,600 pixels and visually checked. Shared operator photographs are identified as shared in captions; they are not claimed as unique branch photographs. Media retains `authorised: false`: publication on an operator website is provenance, not proof of a separate reuse licence. Existing unrelated images were preserved.

## Closure and discovery decisions

- TimberJacks Shrewsbury: Tripadvisor reports permanent closure while indexed operator content still lists it. Retained unverified, with conflicting sources recorded; do not treat its old booking profile as proof of current operation.
- The other TimberJacks branches, The Rage House, The Snake Room, All The Rage Birmingham, Splatter Central and The Mad Splatter: current source checks were blocked, unavailable or insufficient. Retained unverified, not labelled closed.
- Lumberjack Cardiff and Swansea: direct requests were blocked, but official pages were readable through web research and still publish branch addresses and activity information. Historical verified state retained.
- BOOM Lab London: current operator content describes six escape rooms; removed the unsupported paint-splatter category and paint-explosion claim.
- Seaside Splatter: the £20 Little Splatters product is marked starting soon; current regular splatter starts at £30.
- Splash Art Republic: added Nottingham, Birmingham and Sheffield only. Lincoln, York, Bristol, Leeds, Leicester and Manchester South are marked coming soon and were excluded.
- UNLEASHED by RemoteAbility: excluded for now; the site labels its build a concept design and distinguishes recurring campaign ideas from confirmed events.
- Previously blacklisted Whistle Punks, Destroy’d, Mission Classified, The Smash Lab and The Underground were not reintroduced.
