## Illustrative economics, not a supplier quote

Assume non-VAT-registered seller, production £9.48 tee / £10.80 magic mug / £8.40 notebook / £7.20 mat (the latter three model 20% supplier tax), supplier delivery £4.99 per item, Stripe standard UK-card fee on the total, and a 3% total-order returns/reprint reserve. No advertising, operator time or overhead is included. Exact tax treatment is unconfirmed. Recalculate net of sales tax and recoverable input tax if VAT registered.

| Product | Customer total | Approx contribution | % of total collected |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tee | £24.98 | £9.19 | 37% |
| Heat-reveal mug | £22.98 | £5.96 | 26% |
| Notebook | £19.98 | £5.49 | 27% |
| Mouse mat | £16.98 | £3.83 | 23% |

Contribution = total collected − production − actual supplier shipping − card fee − reserve. These lower prices trade margin for accessibility. Do not expect £8 on every accessory: approximately £4–£6 under the assumptions above is more realistic. A £1 higher shipping bill reduces contribution by £1. No promised profit, validated unit costs or VAT configuration is implied. Samples, supplier quote and launch setup remain outstanding.
