# RageRoom Directory discovery data-quality audit

Audit date: 19 August 2026  
Scope: all 43 records in `data/listings.json` and every discovery field introduced by the expansion.  
Method: branch-specific official venue pages and booking systems were preferred; authoritative third-party profiles were used only where an official detail page was unavailable. Search snippets were used for discovery, not as the sole basis for a structured claim. Unknown or ambiguous values were set to `null`, `[]`, or omitted.

“All target fields” means activities, occasions, rage-room price and unit, minimum age, session duration and type, group constraints, nullable booking/venue booleans, booking URL, packages and legacy discovery features.

## Quantified corrections

- 43 of 43 venues required at least one data correction, unit classification, explicit-unknown reset, or legacy-feature reconciliation.
- 85 previously populated field assignments or array memberships were removed for insufficient evidence: 38 scalar/collection assignments, 28 occasion memberships, 5 activity memberships and 14 legacy feature memberships.
- 8 activity assignments changed across 6 venues: 5 removed and 3 added.
- 58 occasion assignments changed across 22 venues: 28 removed and 30 added.
- 33 headline price numbers changed across 33 venues: 18 unsupported prices were removed and 15 were replaced with a current rage-specific amount. All 23 retained prices now carry an explicit currency and unit; room/group rates are not treated as per-person rates.

Counts compare the discovery expansion's pre-audit derivation rules and the pre-existing listing values with the evidence-backed dataset. A collection removal counts as one field assignment; individual activity, occasion and legacy-feature memberships are counted separately.
