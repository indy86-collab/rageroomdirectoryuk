# Paint / Splatter inventory audit — 20 August 2026

## Scope and qualification

This audit covers the 15 Paint / Splatter records present at commit `330d3103569a0835ce740bd3b4ff8fd11c22122d`, a keyword review of the other 53 directory records, and a new UK-wide candidate search. A venue qualifies only when customers actively throw, flick, spray, pour or otherwise use paint in a high-energy splatter, action-painting, neon/UV, paint-fight or creative-release experience. Ordinary painting classes, paint-and-sip, pottery painting, conventional crafts and static art experiences do not qualify.

Official venue and booking sources were preferred. Unknown commercial fields remain unknown; prices were retained in the operator's published unit. Fixed branches were verified separately and mobile services were not assigned fabricated coordinates.

Of the 34 final Paint records, 23 have a published price, 18 a safely representable generic minimum age, 27 a published duration, and 29 a direct official booking URL. The remaining values are intentionally unknown rather than estimated.

A 20 August link check followed the effective booking URL, or the source URL where no direct booking URL is published, for all 34 Paint records. Thirty-three destinations returned HTTP 200. Splatter Central's official SumUp Store returned HTTP 403 to command-line traffic while remaining the operator's current official storefront; this anti-bot response is documented rather than replaced with an unverified URL. Rage N Splatter and both mobile operators remain enquiry-led where no direct checkout URL is published.

## Data and modelling notes

- The final set contains 32 fixed venues and two mobile services. Mobile records have no fabricated postcode or coordinates and do not contribute to city landing eligibility.
- Activity-specific age conflicts at The Activity Dome and Rage N Splatter are not collapsed into misleading venue-wide values. Gravesend's conflicting official age copy is also retained as unknown.
- Boom Lab is classified only for its verified paint-bomb finale in this phase; its escape element was not used to broaden Escape inventory.
- New records use fallback imagery when no approved operator asset already existed. No Google Images or unrelated stock imagery was scraped.
- All new venue pages enter the existing sitemap through the central listing pipeline. Filtered query URLs remain canonicalised to and `noindex` beneath the Paint activity page.
- Fixed records continue to use `LocalBusiness` / `EntertainmentBusiness` schema. Mobile records use `Organization` with `areaServed` and no address.
