## Printer handoff

These are concept source images, not certified production-ready files. Send the flat artwork, not the photographic mockup, to the chosen printer for preflight and a sample quote. Keep original PNGs unchanged.

Confirm required pixel dimensions at intended print size, bleed/safe zones, colour profile, acceptable line/texture detail and print method. The generated tee artworks are 1024 × 1536px with opaque dark backgrounds; they need background treatment and adequate resolution for a large chest print. Do not merely relabel resolution metadata as 300 DPI. Request proper vector reconstruction or higher resolution artwork if needed. The notebook needs a supplier-specific spine/back/bleed layout; its current artwork is the front cover only. The mouse mat needs the exact rounded-corner trim template. The mug needs the actual wrap dimensions, handle clearance and heat-reveal coating limits.

Mockups are illustrative: fabric, print scale, notebook binding, stitched mat edges and the mug's printed handle/coverage are not verified supplier features. Use approved supplier mockups or sample photography before live sales. No sample has been manufactured, no exclusivity/trademark clearance is claimed, and no conversion or sales performance has been measured.
