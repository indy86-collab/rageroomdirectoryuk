# Listing image source audit — 2026-08-20

This audit covers the 42 directory records that had no cover image at the start of the review. Thirty-four records now use 31 locally stored assets discovered on the operator's official site (three operator groups legitimately share one image across branches). Eight records remain without an image because their listed official domains currently resolve to unrelated or compromised content.

The images are assigned only through the listing's legacy `image` field. They have **not** been added to `media` or marked `authorised: true`; an image being publicly served by an operator's official site does not by itself establish a separate reuse licence. The source URLs below preserve provenance for rights review and future refreshes.

## Selection notes

- Operator-hosted experience photographs were preferred over logos where the official page exposed one.
- When an official page exposed only its own brand image, that logo was used rather than introducing a third-party image.
- The Newcastle image filename contains “GLASGOW”, but the image rendered on the official Newcastle page identifies the Newcastle branch; the rendered content, not the storage filename, was used for verification.
- Large source files were resized to a maximum dimension of 1,800 pixels without changing their aspect ratio. Original source URLs remain above.
